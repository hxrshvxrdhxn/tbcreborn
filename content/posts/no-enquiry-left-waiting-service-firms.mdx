Chartered accountants, company secretaries, law firms and advisory practices all have the same quiet problem. New enquiries arrive through the website, WhatsApp, email, phone calls and referrals. Some get a reply within the hour. Some wait two days because each partner assumed someone else had picked it up.

The firms that grow fastest are rarely the cheapest. They are the ones that answer first and follow through.

## Response time decides more than you think

When a prospective client sends the same enquiry to three firms, the first sensible reply often wins the conversation. Most firms do not measure how long their replies take, so they cannot tell whether they are fast or slow. They only notice the clients who went elsewhere, and usually put it down to price.

## Three gaps that cost professional firms work

1. **Enquiries with no owner.** If an enquiry is not assigned the moment it arrives, it waits.
2. **Proposals that take days.** Writing each proposal from scratch means it goes out late and looks different every time.
3. **Documents chased by phone.** Bank statements, registers and signed forms requested on WhatsApp, received in pieces, checked by hand, and chased again.

## What a well-run firm does differently

- **Every enquiry gets an owner, a clock and a next step** the moment it arrives, whatever channel it came from.
- **The response clock is visible to everyone.** Nobody needs to be told that an enquiry is two hours old. They can see it.
- **Proposals come from a price list.** Pick the services, the totals are calculated, the engagement terms are attached, and the client signs online.
- **Documents are requested through one secure link.** Each item has a status (requested, received, accepted or rejected with a reason) and is checked when it arrives, for example whether a bank statement covers the full period asked for.
- **Deadlines and renewals are planned ahead.** Statutory due dates and contract renewals are known for each client weeks in advance, not discovered at the last minute.

## The client experience follows

Clients notice when a firm replies quickly, sends a clear proposal the same day, and asks for each document once. It feels organised, because it is. That impression is often what turns a one-off engagement into a long relationship.
