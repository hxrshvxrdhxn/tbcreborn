## 2. Structural & Editorial Rules Observed

1. **Title Length**: All page titles strictly maintained at or under 60 characters with primary keyword upfront and brand suffix.
2. **Meta Descriptions**: Formatted strictly between 140 and 160 characters, written for CTR with clear value propositions and target markets.
3. **Primary Keyword Positioning**: Every page introduces its primary keyword naturally within the first 100 words of body copy.
4. **Header Hierarchy**: Exactly one `<h1>` per page, followed by logical `<h2>` section groupings and `<h3>` component cards.
5. **Schema.org Graphs**: Valid JSON-LD schemas validated across all routes without duplicate tags. Includes `Organization`, `Service`, `SoftwareApplication`, `FAQPage`, and `BreadcrumbList`.
6. **Honest Search/AI Disclaimers**: Explicit statement included on all AEO/SEO/GEO pages stating that search rankings and AI model citations are never guaranteed.
7. **Brand Palette & Tone**: Strict Rolex green (`#0C3D2A`, `#167A4E`, `bg-forest`) and ivory/white background palette. British spelling throughout. Zero uppercase micro-labels. No client names. No prices. No exclamation marks.
